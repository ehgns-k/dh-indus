# Numerals and the signs they pair with (descriptive)

This note collects, for the count–item pairs established by the tests (H3.10, H3.12, F2, H6.1; variable count slot H9.1), the shapes of the signs involved. The shape descriptions are my own reading of the ICIT-keyed sign images (`scripts/fetch_glyphs.py`) and of the printed KP1979 sign list. **What the shapes depict is not established**; the descriptions are given so that later work can formulate testable hypotheses, and they are not used as evidence anywhere in this project.

## Signs that follow particular counts

| Count | Following sign (ICIT / KP) | Shape (description only) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4–9 short strokes | 390, 405, 406, 407 / 86 | an upright stem with a forked top: three prongs (390) or more branched prongs (405–407) | H3.10, H3.11, R1, R2, F2 |
| 7 short strokes | 585, 575 / 288, 289 | 585: a structure with sloping sides and legs; 575: an upright frame with a small box on top | H3.12a, R1 |
| 3 long strokes | 520 / 217 | an upright shaft with a triangular head (arrow- or spear-like) | H3.12b, R1 |

Short and long strokes take disjoint sets of these partners on post-1979 inscriptions (H6.1; the result depends largely on 033).

## Status

These pairings are structural results (L1). A reading such as "N arrows" or "N branches" would be a semantic hypothesis (L2) and would need a test against information outside the texts, which the available data have not allowed (see `reports/PHASE5_7_MEANING_AND_SOUND.md`).
