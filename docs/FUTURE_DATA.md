# Data needed to decide the open questions

This document lists what new material would be needed to settle the questions that this project could not settle. The figures come from the power simulations PW1–PW4 (`docs/JOURNAL.md`, thirty-fourth session; `results/batch15_power.json`). They assume one-sided Fisher tests at the confirmatory thresholds and 80 % power. "Observed" uses the effect sizes in the present corpus, which are optimistic because the associations were selected for their size. "Halved" halves the log odds ratio.

| Question | Data needed | N (observed) | N (halved) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Closing sign by object class (C2, six associations) | new seal and tablet inscriptions with object type recorded | 400–1,600 | 800 to > 6,400 |
| Sign class absent from copper tablets (H9.4) | new inscriptions with object type, including copper tablets | 800 | 3,200 |
| 405 on unicorn seals of variant W (MO2b lead) | new unicorn seals with the ICIT variant coding | 400 | 6,400 |
| Replication of the jar-sign result (CT15) | new seal and tablet texts containing the jar sign | 400 | 800 |

Other requirements, from the earlier work:

- **Sound values (L3).** No phonetic method tried here could identify even a known language hidden as a cipher, at up to 8,000 lines (E3–E3d). A future attempt needs an independent constraint: a bilingual inscription, names attested in external sources, or pictographic values fixed in advance. It must also show, with known scripts hidden as ciphers, that the method can identify the right language.
- **Writing or not (G3).** None of the statistics tried separates writing from non-linguistic systems (D2, D2b, CT19, D7–D10). More reference corpora of both kinds, of a size comparable to the Indus corpus, would be needed to calibrate such tests.
- **Codes of the source.** The meanings of the ICIT `cult` codes, the `Bull1` suffixes and the KP1979 type and line codes are not documented in accessible sources (`docs/OBJECT_CODES.md`). They should be checked against the printed corpora (CISI; Wells 2011) before further use.
- **Readings of the numeral faces.** The counts on the numeral faces of Harappa tablets (FACE2) should be checked against the photographs in CISI 3.1.
