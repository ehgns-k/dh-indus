## 1. Supported

## 3. Review

- **R7.** The family-wise review now covers 47 tests on fresh or strict held-out data.
  - Holm at α = 0.01 keeps 13 of them: the direction test; the closing-cluster order; the count–item pairs; the positional template; the recurring units; and the nested structure.
  - CT15, F5, F5k, CT22 and CT22k survive Holm at α = 0.05.
  - An earlier statement that the H9.4 association survives Holm at α = 0.01 was withdrawn for the larger family.
- A registration error (AR1: a rule and its examples disagreed) led to a new method rule. Rules take precedence, and the examples' reading is run as well.

## 4. Position

The level assessment is unchanged: L1 achieved, L2 only in the narrow functional form of CT15, and L3 not achieved. The external variables available in the source have now all been tried: object type, motif and its details, material, size, chronological level and excavation area. Apart from object type (CT15), none is predicted by the signs on unused data.
